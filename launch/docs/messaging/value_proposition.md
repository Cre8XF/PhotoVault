# PIXTR – Value Proposition

## Core Value

PIXTR is a private, secure place for your photos — designed to preserve memories without noise, ads, or compromise.

## Primary Value Pillars

### 1. Your photos, truly yours

PIXTR is built around ownership. Your photos are never used for ads, profiling, or AI training. What you upload stays yours.

### 2. Calm by design

No clutter. No distractions. PIXTR focuses on clarity, simplicity, and ease — so your photos remain the center of attention.

### 3. Privacy you can trust

With client-side encryption and Secure Vault, your most private moments are protected by design, not promises.

### 4. Effortless organization

Albums, memories, and intuitive browsing make it easy to find, relive, and enjoy your photos — without work.

### 5. Editing without friction

Simple, powerful tools let you enhance photos naturally, without complexity or learning curves.

## One-Line Summary

A calm, private photo space — built to protect and preserve what matters most.
