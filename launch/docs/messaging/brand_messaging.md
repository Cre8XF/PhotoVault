# Pixtr – Brand Messaging & Voice

## Brand Essence

Pixtr is a calm, privacy-first photo app.
It is designed for people who care about their photos — and want full control over them.

Pixtr does not compete on noise, social features, or attention.
It competes on trust, clarity, and long-term reliability.

---

## Core Brand Promise

> Your photos are yours.
> Stored securely. Viewed calmly. Controlled by you.

---

## Brand Values

### Privacy by default

Privacy is not a feature — it is the foundation.
Pixtr minimizes data exposure and avoids unnecessary tracking.

### Control and ownership

Users control their photos, albums, and Vault.
No hidden behavior. No unclear storage logic.

### Calm experience

Pixtr avoids clutter, distractions, and urgency.
The interface is designed to feel stable and quiet.

### Transparency

Security, storage, and limitations are explained clearly.
No exaggerated claims. No fine-print surprises.

---

## Brand Personality

Pixtr should feel:

- Calm
- Professional
- Trustworthy
- Minimal
- Premium (but not luxury-focused)

Pixtr should **not** feel:

- Playful
- Social-media driven
- Trend-focused
- Sales-heavy
- Overly technical in public copy

---

## Tone of Voice

### General tone

- Confident, but never aggressive
- Clear, but not verbose
- Professional, not corporate
- Reassuring, not emotional

### Writing principles

- Short sentences
- Simple language
- No marketing buzzwords
- No hype or urgency language

### Avoid phrases like

- “Revolutionary”
- “Next-generation”
- “Game-changing”
- “Best ever”
- “Unlimited freedom”

---

## Key Messaging Pillars

### 1. Photo Management

Pixtr helps users organize, browse, and relive their photos — without distraction.

Example phrasing:

- “All your photos, organized”
- “Browse your photos effortlessly”
- “Relive your memories”

---

### 2. Editing

Editing is powerful, but never overwhelming.

Example phrasing:

- “Edit with ease”
- “Simple tools, full control”
- “Enhance your photos without complexity”

---

### 3. Security & Vault

Vault is an optional, advanced layer for private content.

Key points:

- Client-side encryption
- Password never leaves the device
- Photos cannot be recovered if password is lost

Example phrasing:

- “Your private photos. Protected.”
- “Secure Vault with client-side encryption”
- “Only you can access your Vault photos”

---

## Store Messaging Guidelines

### App Store & Play Store

- Lead with clarity, not features
- Mention privacy early
- Keep descriptions factual
- Avoid comparisons with competitors

### Screenshots

- One clear message per screenshot
- No technical jargon
- Calm, readable typography

---

## Website Messaging Guidelines

The website should:

- Reinforce trust
- Explain Vault clearly
- Avoid feature overload
- Feel stable and professional

---

## Internal Rule of Thumb

If a sentence feels like it belongs in an ad — rewrite it.
If a sentence would worry a privacy-conscious user — clarify it.

Pixtr speaks calmly, because it has nothing to hide.
