# PIXTR – Tone of Voice

## Overall Tone

Premium · Calm · Trustworthy · Minimal

PIXTR speaks with confidence, clarity, and restraint.
We do not shout. We do not exaggerate. We let trust speak for itself.

## Key Principles

### Calm, not loud

- Avoid hype, buzzwords, and urgency
- Short sentences, clear meaning
- Space matters — visually and linguistically

### Premium, not flashy

- Professional and polished language
- Subtle confidence over bold claims
- No emojis, no slang, no trend-driven phrasing

### Privacy-first language

- Reassuring, factual, transparent
- No fear-based messaging
- Emphasize control and ownership

### Human and respectful

- Speak to the user, not at them
- Assume intelligence and intent
- Never condescending, never technical without reason

## Language Guidelines

**Use:**

- Clear, simple sentences
- Neutral, timeless wording
- Benefit-driven phrasing

**Avoid:**

- Marketing clichés
- Over-promising
- Aggressive calls to action
- Technical jargon unless necessary

## Example

**Instead of:**
“Military-grade security to protect your data at all costs!”

**Use:**
“Your private photos are protected with secure, client-side encryption.”
