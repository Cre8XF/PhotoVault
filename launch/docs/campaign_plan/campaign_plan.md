# Pixtr – Launch Campaign Plan

## Overview

This document defines the launch strategy for Pixtr.
The goal is a controlled, professional release focused on trust, clarity, and long-term credibility rather than aggressive growth.

Pixtr positions itself as a **premium, privacy-first photo app**.

---

## Launch Objectives

### Primary goals

- Establish trust and legitimacy
- Clearly communicate privacy-first design
- Validate store listings and screenshots
- Achieve a stable first release without review issues

### Secondary goals

- Collect early user feedback
- Validate onboarding and Vault understanding
- Prepare foundation for future growth

---

## Target Audience

### Core audience

- Privacy-conscious users
- Users dissatisfied with ad-driven photo services
- Users who want a calm, premium photo experience

### Not targeting (initially)

- Social-first content creators
- Influencer-driven audiences
- Mass-market growth campaigns

---

## Positioning

### Core message

> Your photos, stored securely and privately — without compromise.

### Supporting pillars

- Privacy-first by design
- Client-side encrypted Vault
- Clean and calm user experience
- No ads, no tracking, no noise

---

## Launch Phases

### Phase 1 – Store Launch (Soft Launch)

**Status:** Initial release

Focus:

- App Store & Google Play approval
- Screenshots and descriptions finalized
- Privacy Policy and Terms published
- Vault explanation clearly communicated

Channels:

- App Store
- Google Play
- Website landing page

Success criteria:

- Approved on both stores
- No review rejections related to privacy or encryption
- Screenshots clearly understood by users

---

### Phase 2 – Controlled Visibility

**Status:** Post-approval

Focus:

- Low-volume visibility
- Brand consistency
- Feedback collection

Channels:

- Website
- Direct sharing
- Limited social presence (optional)

Actions:

- Publish landing page
- Optional announcement post
- Monitor onboarding and Vault usage

---

### Phase 3 – Iteration & Refinement

**Status:** Ongoing

Focus:

- Improve clarity based on real user behavior
- Refine onboarding text
- Optimize screenshots if needed

Actions:

- Adjust store text if confusion is observed
- Improve help text around Vault and security
- Prepare minor updates if necessary

---

## Key Assets

### Store assets

- App Store screenshots (6)
- Google Play screenshots (6)
- Feature graphic
- Store descriptions and keywords

### Legal & trust assets

- Privacy Policy
- Terms of Service
- Security and Vault explanations

### Web assets

- Landing page
- Privacy and Terms pages

---

## Metrics to Monitor

### Store metrics

- Conversion from store view → install
- Screenshot engagement (qualitative)
- Review feedback

### In-app signals

- Vault setup completion rate
- Onboarding completion
- Early drop-off points

---

## Risk Management

### Identified risks

- Misunderstanding of Vault password recovery
- App Store concerns regarding encryption
- Users expecting social features

### Mitigation

- Clear Vault explanations
- Review notes submitted to Apple
- Conservative feature messaging

---

## Long-Term Outlook

Pixtr is positioned for slow, steady growth.
Trust, clarity, and stability are prioritized over short-term adoption.

Future campaigns may include:

- Expanded editor features
- Additional privacy tooling
- Paid tiers (if applicable)

---

## Status

This campaign plan is considered **active and stable** for the initial Pixtr launch.
