# Pixtr – Google Play Data Safety Cheat Sheet

This document maps Pixtr’s actual behavior to the Google Play Data Safety form.

---

## Does the app collect or share user data?

**Yes – limited data collection.**
**No data is shared with third parties.**

---

## Data Collected

### Personal Information

- **Email address**
  - Purpose: Account management
  - Optional: No
  - Collected: Yes
  - Shared: No

### Photos and Videos

- **User-generated content**
  - Purpose: Core app functionality (photo storage)
  - Optional: Yes (user chooses what to upload)
  - Collected: Yes
  - Shared: No

### App Activity

- **App interactions**
  - Purpose: Core app functionality
  - Optional: No
  - Collected: Yes
  - Shared: No

---

## Data NOT Collected

- Location data
- Contacts
- Messages
- Call logs
- Browsing history
- Advertising identifiers
- Device identifiers for tracking
- Financial information
- Health data

---

## Data Sharing

- Pixtr **does not share data** with third parties.
- No data is sold, rented, or used for advertising.

---

## Data Security Practices

### Is data encrypted in transit?

**Yes**

### Is data encrypted at rest?

**Yes**

### Secure Vault

- Vault photos use **client-side encryption**
- Encryption occurs on the user’s device
- Pixtr cannot access or decrypt Vault content

---

## Data Deletion

- Users can delete their account at any time
- Upon deletion, associated data is permanently removed
- Vault photos cannot be recovered after deletion

---

## Data Usage

- Data is used only to provide core app functionality
- No tracking, profiling, or advertising usage

---

## Children

- Pixtr is **not directed at children**
- Target audience: General audience (13+)

---

## Summary (for reviewer confidence)

Pixtr is a privacy-first photo storage app.
User data is collected only as required to provide the service and is never
shared or used for advertising or profiling.
