Privacy Policy – Pixtr

Last updated: January 2026

Pixtr respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your data is handled when you use the Pixtr application.

1. Data collection
Pixtr does not sell or share personal data with third parties for advertising purposes.

The app may process the following data to function correctly:
- Account information (email, authentication identifiers)
- Photos and albums uploaded by the user
- App usage data strictly required for functionality

2. Photos and content
All photos uploaded to Pixtr belong to the user.

Photos stored in the Secure Vault are encrypted on the user’s device using client-side encryption. Pixtr cannot access, view, or recover Vault photos.

3. Encryption and security
- Vault photos are encrypted locally on your device
- Vault passwords are never stored or transmitted
- If a Vault password is lost, encrypted photos cannot be recovered

4. Biometric authentication
Pixtr may use biometric authentication (Face ID / Touch ID / fingerprint) if enabled by the user. Biometric data is handled by the operating system and never accessed by Pixtr.

5. Data storage
Photos may be stored on secure cloud infrastructure to allow synchronization across devices. Vault encryption is applied before upload.

6. Account deletion
Users may delete their account at any time. Upon deletion, associated data is permanently removed, except where retention is required by law.

7. Changes
This policy may be updated. Continued use of Pixtr indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
