# Apple App Review Notes – Pixtr

Thank you for reviewing Pixtr.

Pixtr is a privacy-first photo storage application designed with a calm,
transparent, and secure user experience.

Below is a clear explanation of security-related features to assist with review.

---

## Secure Vault & Encryption

Pixtr includes an optional feature called **Secure Vault**, intended for private photos.

Vault photos are protected using **client-side encryption**:

- Encryption is performed locally on the user’s device
- Encrypted photos are uploaded only after encryption is applied
- Pixtr cannot access, view, or decrypt Vault photos

---

## Password Handling

- Vault passwords are **never stored, transmitted, or logged**
- Pixtr has no technical ability to recover Vault passwords
- If a user forgets their Vault password, encrypted Vault photos **cannot be recovered**
- This behavior is intentional and clearly communicated to users

---

## Biometric Authentication

Pixtr supports biometric authentication (Face ID / Touch ID / fingerprint) as an **optional convenience feature**:

- Biometrics are handled entirely by the operating system
- Pixtr does not access or store biometric data
- A Vault password or PIN must be set before biometric unlock can be enabled

---

## Data Access & Privacy

- Pixtr does not sell or share user data
- Photos belong exclusively to the user
- Vault content is inaccessible to Pixtr by design
- No tracking, profiling, or advertising usage of photos

---

## Account Deletion

Users can delete their account at any time.
Upon deletion:

- Associated data is permanently removed
- Vault photos cannot be recovered after deletion

---

## Purpose of the App

Pixtr is designed as a personal photo storage and organization tool.
It does not include social features, public sharing, or content discovery.

---

If further clarification is needed, we are happy to provide it.

Thank you for your time and review.
