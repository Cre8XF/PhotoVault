## Secure Vault for private photos

Some photos are meant for you alone.

Pixtr includes an optional **Secure Vault** for sensitive content.
Vault photos are protected using client-side encryption, applied directly on
your device.

- Only you can access your Vault photos
- Vault passwords are never stored or transmitted
- Lost passwords cannot be recovered — by design

**Your private photos. Protected.**
