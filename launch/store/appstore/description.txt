Pixtr is a premium photo storage app built for people who value both simplicity and privacy.

Keep all your photos in one place, organize them into albums, relive memories from previous years, and edit your images with ease.

Pixtr includes a secure Vault for sensitive photos. Vault photos are encrypted on your device using client-side encryption. Your password is never stored or transmitted.

Lost Vault passwords cannot be recovered.

Pixtr is designed to give you full control — without compromise.
