Pixtr is a secure and elegant photo storage app designed for people who care about their memories and their privacy.

Store all your photos in one place, organize them into albums, relive moments from previous years, and edit your images with simple, intuitive tools. Pixtr is built with a clean interface, smooth performance, and a calm premium experience.

Key features:
- All your photos, one place
- Smart albums & browsing
- Memories from previous years
- Simple photo editing
- Secure Vault for private photos

Privacy & security:
Pixtr uses client-side encryption for Vault photos. Your Vault password never leaves your device. Biometric unlock and auto-lock are supported. Vault photos are hidden from the main gallery.

If you forget your Vault password, encrypted photos cannot be recovered — by design.
